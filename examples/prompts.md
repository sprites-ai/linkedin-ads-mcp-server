# Example prompts for the LinkedIn Ads MCP server

These work as written in Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Codex, Gemini CLI and the other clients listed in the README. Anything that changes the account comes back as an approval first.

## Read and report

- With Sprites, what is our LinkedIn CPL by job function this quarter?
- Ask Sprites which LinkedIn audiences are saturated and should be rotated.
- With Sprites, compare LinkedIn spend and leads this month against last month.
- Use Sprites to forecast LinkedIn reach and cost per lead for VP Finance at US companies with 1000+ staff.
- Ask Sprites to compare forecasts for three audiences: CFOs, Controllers, and Heads of FP&A in the UK.

## Change, create and launch

- Use Sprites to build an ABM campaign for Series B fintechs in the US, 200 to 1000 employees.
- Have Sprites draft 3 new Sponsored Content intros for our best-performing lead gen form.
- Have Sprites upload this list of 200 target accounts as a LinkedIn company audience.
- With Sprites, cut the LinkedIn budget on campaigns with CPL above $250 by 20%.

## Recurring

- Use Sprites to send me a LinkedIn leads and CPL report every Friday.

## Tips

- Name the campaign, ad set or page in full. Sprites receives only the request, not the earlier chat.
- Say the period ("last 30 days", "this quarter"); your client passes today's date so it resolves correctly.
- Working on several businesses? Say "switch to <business name>" first (`choose_business`).
- To compare channels, ask about them in one request, for example "compare Google and Meta CPA last month".
